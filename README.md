# Orthofit Studio – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Orthofit Studio is a boutique Pilates and personal training studio located in Business Bay, Dubai. It specialises in private Pilates instruction, small-group Reformer classes, prenatal and postnatal programmes, rehabilitation Pilates, and personalised movement training.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the studio's personalised approach, professional expertise, and specialist Pilates services. It aims to help visitors identify the right programme, access essential class information, and enquire or book a suitable session.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Brand Positioning and First Impression
- Strengthened the homepage messaging around personalised Pilates and movement training.
- Made the studio's specialist approach and individual attention more prominent.
- Improved the presentation of its experience, expertise, and key differentiators.
- Addressed the previous lack of clarity about what Orthofit offers and who its services are designed for.

### 2. Accessible Pricing and Class Schedule Information
- Replaced image-dependent information with properly structured, readable HTML content.
- Improved the presentation of pricing and class schedules for easier access across devices.
- Made important class information easier to read, navigate, and search.
- Addressed accessibility and usability limitations caused by displaying essential information through images.

### 3. Clearer Service Categories and Customer Pathways
- Organised private Pilates, Reformer classes, prenatal and postnatal Pilates, and rehabilitation programmes into distinct service categories.
- Improved service descriptions to help visitors understand which programmes match their needs.
- Strengthened relevant enquiry and booking calls to action.
- Addressed the previous lack of clear pathways for customers with different fitness and wellness requirements.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Applied the established brand palette of charcoal grey (#585656), soft green (#9CBE9C), and white to maintain a calm, professional wellness identity.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved content hierarchy to introduce the studio's positioning before presenting services and booking information.
- **Information Accessibility:** Presented essential pricing and scheduling information as readable website content rather than image-based material.
- **User Experience:** Made specialist programmes easier to understand and navigate through clearer service categorisation.
- **Conversion Focus:** Improved the visibility of session enquiries, WhatsApp contact, and booking opportunities.
- **Brand Consistency:** Aligned the visual presentation and messaging with Orthofit's personalised, knowledgeable, and welcoming brand personality.

---

**Project Focus:** Clearer personalised Pilates positioning, accessible pricing and scheduling information, and more effective service discovery and booking pathways.
